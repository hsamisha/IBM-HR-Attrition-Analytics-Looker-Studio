##  Project Overview

The **IBM HR Attrition Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive HR analytics project developed using **Looker Studio** and **Excel**.

The dashboard analyzes employee information and attrition patterns through interactive KPIs, charts, filters, and multiple dashboard pages.

## Objectives

- Analyze employee demographics
- Understand employee attrition patterns
- Analyze employee characteristics
- Identify patterns related to job satisfaction and involvement
- Analyze employee income and job-related factors
- Present HR insights through an interactive dashboard

## Tools & Technologies

- Looker Studio
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Visualization
- Data Analytics

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Contains key HR KPIs and high-level visualizations such as:

- Total Employees
- Employee Attrition
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Monthly Income
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Job Role
- Business Travel Analysis

### 2. Employee Analysis

This page analyzes employee characteristics using:

- Age Analysis
- Gender Analysis
- Education Analysis
- Job Level Analysis
- Marital Status Analysis
- Monthly Income Analysis

### 3. Attrition Analysis

This page focuses on factors associated with employee attrition:

- Attrition by Overtime
- Attrition by Job Satisfaction
- Attrition by Environment Satisfaction
- Attrition by Job Involvement
- Attrition by Years at Company
- Attrition by Business Travel

### 4. Summary

Provides a summarized view of the major HR analysis and dashboard insights.

## Key KPIs

- Total Employees
- Employee Attrition
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Monthly Income
